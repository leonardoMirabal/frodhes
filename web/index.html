FRODHES

Tap "Start sound" (browsers need a tap before they play audio).

QUICK GUIDE

One page at a time, with four cards: KNOB 1–4 turn the four cards you see. SELECT turns the pages, or press a button to jump to its page (press it again on its page for the second action).

Play

  • PRESETS knob: the sounds (F01–F12 factory, U01–U16 yours). ALGORITHM: the Rhodes type.
  • PERFORM (the first page, HOME): K1–K4 are the effects' dry / wet. Choose what they do on ASSIGN.
  • REC is the sustain pedal (hold, or tap to latch). OCT− / OCT+: octave.

Buttons

  • HOME PERFORM ↔ the main page (type, drive, reverb, touch) · EDIT the voice (tone, bark, bell, hammer)
  • ENV decay, again: compressor · LFO tremolo / wah · SEL chorus — on their page: on / off
  • FX all effects off / on · SAVE store a preset (hold: undo a load)
  • SEQ chord mode, again: the sequencer · ARP the arpeggiator (again: on / off)
  • GLO tempo, again: setup, again: MIDI · PLAY tap tempo (ARPEGGIO: hold; SEQUENCER: start / stop)

Sequencer

On its page: SEQ = record (playing: overdub; stopped: step by step), PLAY = start / stop, hold SEQ = clear. ALGORITHM moves a cursor step by step, PRESETS bar by bar; K4 EDIT: undo / delete / erase.

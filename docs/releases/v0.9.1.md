**Frodhes 0.9.1**: a physically modelled Fender Rhodes electric piano for the M-VAVE FM-1. Every note is computed live from the mechanics of a Rhodes (hammer, tine, tonebar, pickup); there are no samples.

**Download:** `frodhes-0.9.1.fwsc` below. **Try it first in your browser:** https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/

### Install
```sh
pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py frodhes-0.9.1.fwsc
```
If anything goes wrong, switch the FM-1 off, hold **OCT−** and switch it on to get the USB rescue, then install again.

### New in 0.9.1
- **TRS MIDI In** ([#1](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/issues/1)): the FM-1's 3.5 mm TRS MIDI input works, for DAWless setups. Notes, CCs, program changes and MIDI clock come in exactly as over USB.
- **MIDI page** (press GLO three times): **CHANNEL** (ALL or 1–16, for USB and TRS alike; the FM-1 sends its notes on it), **TRS IN** on / off, **CLOCK**.
- **Sequencer**, a pattern recorder on the SEQ button:
  - Play notes or chords and they loop over 1–16 bars.
  - **QUANT** (off to 1/16T) and **SWING** apply as the pattern plays, so you can change them at any time.
  - Record live with overdub, or **step by step while it's stopped**: notes go on the cursor's step, and holding the keys while turning ALGORITHM makes them longer.
  - **UNDO** the last pass, **ERASE** by holding keys, **DELETE** a step, or hold SEQ to clear everything.
  - The screen shows what plays now and next in big letters, plus the bar as 16 cells. ALGORITHM moves step by step and PRESETS bar by bar.
- **Chord mode** (CHORD page, SEQ button): one key plays a chord. Use DIATONIC to follow the SCALE page, or pick a fixed shape (MAJ, MIN7, SUS4…), with inversion, voicing and strum.
- The phaser is now reached with SELECT (or its PERFORM knob), as the SEQ button opens CHORD and SEQUENCER.

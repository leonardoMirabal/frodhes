**Frodhes 0.9.10** makes the electric piano cleaner at the hardest strikes and longer-ringing, with a quicker damper; gives RESPONSE two settings, FULL (the whole dynamic range) and LIGHT (a light touch); keeps every effect from adding level peaks; adds a second FM-1 image that plays 14 voices on the chip's two cores; and brings the same sound to computers as a plugin for macOS, Windows and Linux.

**Download:** `frodhes.fwsc` below (the same file as `frodhes-0.9.10.fwsc`; the newest is always at https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/releases/latest/download/frodhes.fwsc). **Try it first in your browser:** https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/

### Install

**FM-1**: download `frodhes.fwsc` below. Install it from the [emulator page](https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/#install) (Chrome or Edge, **Install on your FM-1 or SMK-37**), or from a terminal:

```sh
pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py frodhes.fwsc
```

Your settings, presets and pattern are kept. If anything goes wrong, switch the FM-1 off, hold **OCT−** and switch it on: that is the USB rescue, and you can install again from there.

**FM-1, two cores**: `frodhes-c1.fwsc` below, installed from a terminal (`python3 tools/fm1_install.py frodhes-c1.fwsc`), plays 14 voices (the voices on one core, the effects on the other; the sound 5.8 ms later). Install either image over the other at any time; your settings, presets and pattern are kept.

**Computer (plugin)**: `frodhes-plugin-macos.pkg` (or `frodhes-plugin-macos.zip`), `frodhes-plugin-windows.zip`, `frodhes-plugin-linux-x86_64.zip` or `frodhes-plugin-linux-aarch64.zip` below. How to install each: [PLUGIN.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/PLUGIN.md#packages).

**Move**: you need Schwung on the Move. Install Frodhes with Schwung Manager in a web browser: **Install Custom Module > From GitHub URL**, `leonardoMirabal/frodhes`. Or download `frodhes-module.tar.gz` below and use **From Tarball**. Details: [the Move manual](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/MOVE.md#install).

**SMK-37 Pro, SMK-37 Elite, MKE-P37**: `frodhes-smk37-pro.fwsc`, `frodhes-smk37-elite.fwsc` and `frodhes-mke-p37.fwsc` below. Read [SMK37.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/SMK37.md) first.

### New in 0.9.10
- **A two-core image for the FM-1**, `frodhes-c1.fwsc`, next to the usual `frodhes.fwsc`. It runs the voices on
  the chip's first core and every effect on the second: 14 voices on every instrument (10 on the one-core image),
  and the REVERB TYPEs at the full sample rate, as on the Move. The sound reaches the outputs one audio block
  (5.8 ms) later than on the one-core image. Everything else is the same: the pages, the presets, your settings and
  pattern (install either one over the other at any time). `frodhes.fwsc` stays the default.
- **Frodhes as a plugin for computers**: the FM-1's sound engine, compiled unchanged, as a CLAP, VST3 and Audio
  Unit instrument on macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and a CLAP and VST3 instrument on Windows (x64) and Linux
  (x86_64 and ARM64). It plays the high-quality version of every instrument and effect, with 24 voices on every
  instrument; the factory presets F01-F57 and your own preset files, UNDO, program changes, MIDI CHANNEL, the
  velocity settings, the tempo source, MIDI learn, SUSTAIN latch and the effects bypass, all saved with the project.
- It runs at any sample rate the host uses (22.05 to 192 kHz) and tells the host its latency (258 samples at
  44.1 kHz, 325 at 48 kHz), so the host keeps it in time with the other tracks.
- On macOS it has its own window: the FM-1's pages as cards, PRESETS (every preset, factory and user, grouped by
  instrument), SAVE with a name field, bypassed effects' knobs drawn inactive, the window's size saved with the
  project. On Windows and Linux the host shows its generic parameter view. The SETTINGS sheet shows the version.
- The packages: `frodhes-plugin-macos.pkg` (or `.zip`), `frodhes-plugin-windows.zip`,
  `frodhes-plugin-linux-x86_64.zip` and `frodhes-plugin-linux-aarch64.zip`. Each is self-contained (nothing else to
  install). The macOS installer is not signed by a registered developer: open it once from System Settings >
  Privacy & Security > Open Anyway. How to install each: [PLUGIN.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/PLUGIN.md#packages).
- DISPLAY 2, a new page right after DISPLAY (SELECT one page on): K1 **SUSTAIN LEDS**. With LEDS on ALL or SCALE, a
  key let go while the sustain keeps its note ringing stays dark until the sound stops (**DARK**, the default, as
  before) or lights again at once (**LIT**), so the map of the keys stays readable with the sustain on (issue #35).
  Kept with the settings; LEDS PLAYED and ROOTS are unchanged. The SMK-37 has no key lights and no DISPLAY 2 page.

### Changes
- Electric piano, the hardest strikes: notes near velocity 127 no longer overdrive the pickup. Above a knee that
  depends on the key and TYPE, the tine's swing is cut a little more with each velocity step (up to about 22 dB at
  v127 on the DYNO top keys, none on most FELT keys) and the output gain takes it back, so the fundamental keeps
  its level. The second harmonic at v127 is now at or below -12 dB re the fundamental on every TYPE, sustain and
  attack (0.9.9: up to -5 dB sustain and -2 dB attack on DYNO, -8 / -4 dB on STAGE 77 and SUITCASE). v127 is still
  louder than v110 (+3.4 to +3.9 LU K-weighted) and brighter (the attack's spectral centroid higher). Every velocity
  under the knee, and VIBES and MARIMBA, sound exactly as before.
- Electric piano decay: held notes in the middle and upper register ring longer, closer to a real tine piano. The
  fundamental's T60 (v40 / v64, host) is now about 19-20 s from C3 to A4 (was 10-11 s), 14 s at C5 (was 8 s),
  4.2 s at A6 (was 2.2 s); C#2-D#2 a little shorter (23-25 s, was 27 s). DECAY still scales every key the same way.
- Electric piano damper: letting go of a key stops the note faster and sooner. The release T60 is 0.25 s in the bass,
  0.18 s in the middle, 0.14 s and 0.12 s in the treble (was 0.5 / 0.4 / 0.3 / 0.2 s), and the felt lands within a few
  ms (its settle is 2.2-5.1 ms by register, was 3.6-7.3 ms): a note falls 10 dB in 25-45 ms (was 55-95 ms). RELEASE
  scales it x0.25 (0) to x8 (127): 127 keeps the long releases of before (about 2 s in the bass, 1.4 s at C4, 1 s at
  E6). Release velocity, the pedal and the
  half pedal work as before, on the new times.
- DRIVE TSH8 and OHCD on quiet bass notes: both kept one internal signal in whole units, and on a soft low note its
  steps were a faint buzz of odd harmonics. TSH8's clipper output now carries 4 more bits into the tone stage (the
  same instructions on its longest paths); OHCD's first stage runs 16 times finer inside (2 instructions a sample,
  its budget 160 us a block, under the drive's 177). A 41 Hz sine through either at DRIVE 20: the 3rd harmonic
  re the fundamental falls from -44 / -50 / -59 dB to -67 / -79 / -90 dB (TSH8, inputs 160 / 320 / 640) and from
  -38 / -54 / -57 dB to -54 / -71 / -79 dB (OHCD, inputs 40 / 160 / 320). Loud playing is unchanged. On F05 with
  TSH8, key 28 at velocity 20: the 3rd harmonic is now -60 dB re H1 (0.9.9: -46). OHCD and BHLUE there sit on the
  output's own floor (harmonics near -115 dBFS).
- DRIVE TONE with the compressor on (every factory preset has it): the compressor sits before the drive and now
  holds its detector over a note's cycle, so on loud chords it feeds the drive about 0.5 dB less. On such a chord
  TONE's lower half (0 to the middle) moves the treble at 8 kHz by about 2.6 dB (0.9.9: 5.7) and its upper half by
  3.5 dB (0.9.9: 4.2). With the compressor off, TONE's range is 0.9.9's (lower half 9.5 dB, upper half 4.5 dB).
  TONE's filter itself is unchanged.
- **FX** (the bypass, CC 111) keeps the compressor on: it switches off every other effect. The bypass had dropped the
  compressor and its make-up, so FX off sounded 1.0-1.2 dB weaker on F01-F04 (up to 2.6 dB on other factory
  presets) and changed the piano's dynamics. With every other effect at depth 0, FX on and off now match within
  0.5 dB in level and in width (both mono), on the one-core and the two-core builds. The compressor's meter stays live
  while bypassed. (The Move had this already, `FR_BYP_KEEP_CMP`; it is now the default everywhere, `-DFR_BYP_KEEP_CMP=0`
  for the old bypass.)
- **The output level is 0.9.9's again** (the default LEVEL and the limiter's threshold, LIM_T 24000): the factory
  presets within 0.5 dB of 0.9.9 by loudness (the VIBES and MARIMBA presets, below, excepted), on the FM-1, the Move
  and the plugin.
- **No effect adds peak level.** Each effect switched on keeps the loudest moments within 2 dB of the dry sound
  (`tools/fx_pristine.py`'s new peak check), so none of them drives the output limiter:
  - PHASER: its level trimmed by DEPTH so a chord's peak stays within 0.5 dB of the phaser off (single notes within
    2 dB); DEPTH 0 matches by loudness, the deep settings are 2-3 dB quieter on average. The sound itself is unchanged
    (a pure gain).
  - PAN at DEPTH 127: neither side goes above unity (it had a +3 dB side peak).
  - Touch wah: the make-up capped at twice the ratio of the input's and output's peaks, so it no longer overshoots a
    note's onset (+1.6 dB at most, +7 dB before) and a soft high note under a closed resonant wah keeps its level.
  - Filter LP12 / LP18: resonance gain compensation as the cutoff falls (a resonant sweep stays within about 1 dB of
    the unfiltered level).
  - ANA delay: on a silent line its time no longer glides from the last setting, which had bunched the first repeats
    together (a chord's peak +3.1 dB before, +1.7 now; the echoes as loud as DIG's).
  - HQ HALL (Move / plugin) at MIX 127: the switch between its quiet and loud paths moved lower, so its non-linear
    residual falls from -73.7 to -76.4 dBFS.
- **The compressor is clean on chords.** It holds its detector over a note's cycle (11.6 ms), smooths its reduction's
  release (about 0.1 s, never slower than half the RELEASE setting) and interpolates its gain: a v127 chord's
  intermodulation with FX off (the compressor stays on) at most -68.9 dB on every TYPE (was -45 .. -56; the limit -60).
  Loud v127 chords sit about 0.45 dB lower; v64 / v96 within 0.15 dB. Its gain no longer adds a faint 3rd harmonic to
  quiet high notes (the result rounded with a 1-bit dither from -60 dBFS up, not cut: FX off, a soft A6 on BELL DYNO
  had its 3rd harmonic 5 dB up).
- Factory presets with effects re-levelled for the quieter effects: F05 +7, F09 +14, F10 +2, F11 +21, F18 +4 LEVEL
  steps (the Move's preset records the same); U04 +3, U05 +9, U07 +10. Then F10 +5, F12 +9, F19 +8 to 0.9.9's v127
  chord and note level (F12 and F19 had lost 2-3 dB with the clean top register).
- **No factory preset reaches the limiter on normal playing.** Four-note velocity-127 chords with the pedal stay at
  least 0.5 dB under the output limiter on every preset; the VIBES and MARIMBA presets reached it and play quieter
  now: F13 VIBES LEVEL 55 -> 45, F14 VIBES ECHO 58 -> 48, F15 MARIMBA 59 -> 41, F16 WOOD ECHO 41 -> 25 (the Move's
  variants by the same measure). Against 0.9.9 (which reached these levels through the limiter, F15's and F16's chords
  at its ceiling): F13 -2.4, F14 -2.5, F15 -3.2, F16 -6.0 dB on a chord.
- F02 +2, F03 +1, F05 +1, F06 +2 LEVEL steps: their loud chords back with F01's (within 1.3 dB of 0.9.9).
- A filter TYPE change no longer clicks with RESO up (the compensation is applied on every TYPE, so the crossfade's two
  TYPEs share one state).
- FM-1 keys, TOUCH LIGHT / MEDIUM / HEAVY: velocity 127 is kept for the hardest hits. Each key learns its firmest
  landing (its floor). A press as firm as that now plays KEY VEL + 0.75 x (127 - KEY VEL) (119 at KEY VEL 96), and
  127 needs a press clearly firmer (half an octave of the reading under the floor). Before, every press at or under
  the floor played 127.
- KEY VEL above 107: the medium press plays at most 107, so a harder press always has 20 steps to 127. The extra KEY
  VEL lifts the soft presses instead (at KEY VEL 127 the lightest press plays 21). Before, at KEY VEL 127 every press
  firmer than a medium one played 127: 54 % of the presses in a free-playing record. Replayed through the new curve,
  0 % play 127 and the median is 109. FIXED, MIDI notes and the SMK-37's keybed are unchanged.
- The dry-distortion gate (`tools/dry_dist.py`) also measures the attack (aH2..aH6, 0..150 ms) and the FM-1's keys
  through the key path at their hardest. H2 is now held to 0.9.9 + 1.5 dB.
- SMK-37 (Pro, Elite, MKE-P37): 10 voices on every instrument, as the FM-1 (it was 7): the same chip and the same
  audio budget.
- Electric piano, tine ring: the tine's inharmonic second mode (at 6.3-6.9 times the note) is turned down per key from
  A3 up, so a hard attack in the upper register no longer rings with it: at v127 its band in the first 20 ms is
  -40 dB re the fundamental at C5, -55 at C6 and -65 at C7 (it was -12, -33 and -32; -12 to -24 on C#6-G#6).
  The tables generator fits it per key (`tools/gen_rhodes.py` strike_fit, `params.json` strike_fit); keys below
  A3, the bark and the levels are unchanged.
- RESPONSE (CC 40) has two settings: **FULL** (the default), the full dynamic range, and **LIGHT**, a light touch
  with soft notes louder. FULL goes from velocity 20 to 127 over about 34.5 dB (39.4 dB from velocity 1), in steps
  of at most 0.65 dB a velocity; LIGHT keeps 0.9.9's 25.5 dB. Both play at the same level from medium velocities up,
  with the same tone at every key and velocity. On VIBES and MARIMBA, FULL is their response as in 0.9.9 (unchanged)
  and LIGHT lifts the soft notes by the electric piano's LIGHT-over-FULL amount (+9 dB at velocity 20 and below, 0
  from 60), the same tone.
- On the electric piano TYPEs the output's silence gate opens and closes 6 dB lower (16 LSB, -66 dBFS, was 32; VIBES and MARIMBA keep 32), so FULL's softest notes and
  long tails are not cut sooner than in 0.9.9 (notes faded by the gate within 1 s, keys 21-96: velocity 1 10, 10 8,
  20 5, 30 4, each at most 0.9.9's).
- The pedal's thud follows the note's level as heard (with RESPONSE's soft end): never louder than the note.
- The velocity page: every TYPE's last VOICE page (VOICE 3/3 on the electric piano types and MARIMBA, VOICE 4/4 on
  VIBES) holds RESPONSE, TOUCH and KEY VEL. They were FRODHES K4 (KEY VEL), SETUP K4 (TOUCH) and MIDI K4 (RESPONSE);
  those cards are empty now. The CCs are unchanged.
- Two layers, kept apart: TOUCH and KEY VEL set the FM-1's own keys' velocity only (a MIDI note's velocity reaches
  the engine as sent); RESPONSE changes the sound only, never the velocity (the USB-MIDI out, the sequencer's
  recording and the arpeggiator keep the velocity played).
- Move: RESP has its own page, VELOCITY, right after the VOICE pages (it was on LFO/SETUP), on Frodhes' screen and on
  Schwung's page list alike; standard and HQ (one module).
- SMK-37: TOUCH and KEY VEL on the velocity page too (K6, K7).
- VERSION: the MIDI page's K4 shows the version installed (a release's number; a development build DEV and the
  first digits of its commit), read only. The Move shows it on its last page (MAPPINGS, VER).

### Fixed
- COMPRESSOR MAKEUP: turning it fast (or a jump over MIDI) no longer clicks. The make-up glides to the knob's value
  in a few milliseconds; 0 to 127 in one CC was a +24 dB step over 16 samples.
- DELAY TAPE: turning TIME no longer adds a zipper to the echoes. The playback head's tone (its low bump and its
  high-frequency loss) now follows the tape's real speed as the motor gets there, not the knob at once.
- REVERB HALL: moving SIZE between AUTO and a fixed size (up to 35 steps apart) no longer steps the tail; HALL's size
  glides a quarter step every 0.7 ms.
- REVERB PLATE: turning SIZE under a ringing tail is smooth: the cross-fade between the old and new reads moves every
  sample (it moved in eight steps per read, a faint buzz while SIZE turned), and it now takes 1 s (it was 0.13 s;
  0.25 s on the HQ and two-core builds), so the tail no longer bends in pitch while SIZE turns.
- REVERB PLATE: its modulation no longer adds grain. The two modulated reads in the plate jumped 2 samples each
  time the modulation crossed a whole sample, about 20 times a second. That added a faint gritty noise to every PLATE
  tail, 8 to 10 dB of extra energy at 2 to 12 kHz. The plate now sounds as smooth at any MOD as at MOD 0, and its
  level is unchanged (within 0.1 dB).
- CHORUS / PHASER: switching between them (CC 85) or either on or off now fades over about 0.1 s instead of 46 ms. The
  phaser now sits a few dB under the chorus, and the faster fade put that drop into one step.
- REVERB TYPE: a new TYPE no longer starts on a step. What goes into the new TYPE fades in over 6 ms (it started at
  full level, in the middle of a ringing chord's waveform; INFINITE after PLATE was grainy for its first 0.2 s). The
  old TYPE's fade now ends at silence (it ended at 1/128 of its level, then dropped to 0 in one sample).
- REVERB HALL: a quiet note's tail no longer carries a faint grain. On the FM-1 the hall's diffusers round their
  results instead of cutting them; the high-quality HALL (the Move, the plugin) keeps two more bits inside and rounds
  its taps and its return. A quiet note's residual (what is not the reverb's linear response) is back under 0.9.9's.
- SEQUENCER: a note let go under the sustain (REC, or a MIDI pedal on CC 64) was recorded as long as the key was
  held and played back short. It is now recorded as long as it rang: until the sustain lets it go, or until the
  same note is struck again (issue #34). The sustain itself is still not recorded. With the arpeggiator on, its
  keys are recorded as held, as before.
- SEQUENCER: a note held longer than the whole loop was recorded as the part past one loop (held 2.6 s on a 2 s
  loop: 0.6 s). It now plays the whole loop.

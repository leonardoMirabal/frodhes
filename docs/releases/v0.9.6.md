**Frodhes 0.9.6**: a physically modelled electric piano, vibraphone and marimba for the M-VAVE FM-1. Every note is computed live from the mechanics of the instrument; there are no samples.

**Download:** `frodhes.fwsc` below (the same file as `frodhes-0.9.6.fwsc`; the newest is always at https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/releases/latest/download/frodhes.fwsc). **Try it first in your browser:** https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/

### Install
```sh
pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py frodhes.fwsc
```
If anything goes wrong, switch the FM-1 off, hold **OCT−** and switch it on to get the USB rescue, then install again.

### Changes
- The manual: the SCALE page's MODE and TRANSP act on the FM-1's own keys; notes from USB-MIDI and the TRS jack play
  as sent (a DIATONIC / DIA 7TH chord still takes its root onto the scale, from any source).
- TREM/WAH: on WAH the SHAPE card shows "-" (it does nothing there and keeps its value for TREM and PAN).
- The manual: the compressor woken from OFF keeps the volume of what you play about the same (a quiet, dying tail
  comes up, as under any compressor).
- The browser emulator is published with every release, at the same moment, and shows the version it runs (in the
  title, the heading, the footer and on the emulated screen).
- The emulator's knobs turn more evenly: a trackpad swipe or a mouse wheel notch moves them a step or two (a gentle
  swipe used to jump about 30 steps), and a drag moves one step every 10 pixels.
- The manual: DELAY MIX also lowers the dry sound a little (−1 dB at the middle, −6 dB at the top), as it always
  did; REVERB MIX moves in small steps (DECAY and TONE glide); the drive types match the drive off on played
  phrases, and how a held chord's tail and single notes compare.
- COMPRESSOR: turning THRESH up from OFF starts it at −16 dB (the presets' setting) with the +2.8 dB make-up, so it
  comes on at about the same volume (it started at −0.3 dB with the make-up: 2.8 dB louder); CC 106 does the same
  when THRESHOLD is 0 (it came on without the make-up), and keeps a THRESHOLD already sent with CC 107.

### Fixed
- A note played while every voice is busy is never lost: a stolen voice that died away during its steal fade, or
  more new notes in one audio block than voices, dropped the new note (every engine).
- Moving these under ringing notes, echoes or a reverb tail no longer clicks, steps or bends:
  - the Rhodes TYPE (a short crossfade);
  - REVERB MIX turned down (the tail rang up to 6 dB louder at MIX 0);
  - REVERB MOD (HALL, INFINITE);
  - DRIVE AMOUNT on FDRV and KHLON;
  - TREM <-> WAH;
  - DELAY EFFECT DIG <-> ANA (a crossfade);
  - DELAY TIME on DIG (the old and the new time crossfade) and on ANA (it bends gently, as tape does);
  - a synced delay following a new tempo;
  - the WAH's RATE (the resonance);
  - TAPE's TONE;
  - FX (all effects off) with SPREAD on;
  - LEVEL with FX off;
  - the MASTER knob (it stepped once per audio block).
- PERFORM: turning the DELAY or TREM/WAH knob up from OFF switches on the type you last used, not the first one.
- PLATE: a very hot input (the compressor's full make-up into the plate) no longer overflows its input filter.
- Touch WAH: a very hot input (the compressor's full make-up, the pedal) no longer overflows its filter.
- A SAVE, and the settings' save, with the drive's KHLON and the tremolo on (as F06 with TREM) waited for ever: the
  output was silent, but a tiny idle residue under the silence gate never counted as quiet. Saves go through now.
- REVERB INFINITE and PLATE: turning MIX no longer bends the pitch of the tail. With SIZE on AUTO, MIX also set the
  size of the space, and a size change stretches a ringing tail (a held tone went 50 to 250 cents flat while MIX
  moved). On INFINITE, AUTO is now a fixed 5.0 and MIX sets only the level. On PLATE the AUTO size waits while a tail
  rings and follows MIX again once the plate is silent, so the presets sound as before. HALL is unchanged.
- PHASER: it keeps the level at every DEPTH. At a low DEPTH (0, the default, and up to about 48) the sweep sat
  still in the middle of the sound as a fixed notch: a chord came out about 4 dB quieter and a single A4 almost 8 dB.
  Below the middle of DEPTH the sweep now moves up as it narrows (to the top of its range at 0), and the phaser's
  level follows DEPTH so a chord's loudness matches the phaser switched off everywhere (within 0.1 LU on the host).
  Turning DEPTH glides, without a zipper.
- CHORUS: the bass stays full. The chorus' short delay cancelled part of the lowest notes (C2 about 10 dB quieter
  at DEPTH 64); the chorus now leaves everything below about 200 Hz as it is, and its level is a little steadier
  across DEPTH (a chord within about 1 dB of the chorus off).
- CHORUS: turning DEPTH no longer zippers (the delay tap jumped and the mix stepped with each step of the knob).
- DELAY: turning MIX no longer steps the echoes' level (DIG, ANA and TAPE): it now glides sample by sample.
- Loading a preset while a chord or a tail rings no longer clicks: the effects it switches fade over instead of
  switching in one sample, and the Rhodes TYPE crossfades.
- FILTER: RESO glides; a jump of the cutoff (a MIDI CC 74 jump, a preset) moves at most 1/8 octave a step, so a
  band-pass no longer clicks and rings over; one TYPE to another crossfades on the running filter instead of
  passing through the dry sound (HP12 -> HP18 jumped about 21 dB).
- PHASER: DEPTH and FEEDBK glide; its sweep keeps time while the phaser is off, so it comes back in step.
- Switching effects under a held chord no longer clicks: PHASER and CHORUS on / off, the WAH on / off, the
  COMPRESSOR on / off, every FILTER TYPE change and OFF, DRIVE AMOUNT to 0 and back: each fades over about 46 ms
  with the effect still running (as the drive's and the delay's switches always did).
- LEVEL glides instead of stepping at each detent.

### Tools
- CI builds the emulator on every pull request and runs its selftest in Chromium and WebKit; the release workflow
  builds it from the tag, tests it, and publishes the release only once GitHub Pages serves it.
- `tools/control_sweep.py`: every control (115 of them) swept and moved under a ringing chord, judged for clicks
  (against the same render without the move), zipper, level jumps, pitch and stuck states;
  `tests/py/test_manual_claims.py` checks the manual's promises of glides and steady levels the same way.
- `tools/monkey.py`: seeded random play on the host (notes, the pedal, knobs, buttons, presets, MIDI), with checks
  for lost notes, clicks, overflows (sanitizer builds), silence after all notes off, never louder after the key-up,
  and settings that survive a power cycle; failures are shrunk to a few events. `--focus-xfade` aims at the
  controls with a crossfade, `--shed N` adds the overload's voice shedding. `tools/hil_monkey.py` plays the same
  streams on the FM-1 and judges the USB recording with the glitch detector.
- `tools/coverage.sh` (with `coverage_sum.py`): line and condition coverage of the host tests.
- `tools/hil_usb_bench.py`: a bench build (`APP_DEFS=-DFELUCCA_UAC_TONE=1` or `2`, never in a normal build) that
  sends a known wave through the USB audio path, to tell a lost or repeated USB frame from the music.
- `tools/hil_crackle.py`: a repeated-frame event is counted only where the wave around it is predictable and well
  over the music's own quiet plateaus (fewer false alarms on noisy music; real copies are still found).

**Frodhes 0.9.3**: a physically modelled Fender Rhodes and vibraphone for the M-VAVE FM-1. Every note is computed live from the mechanics of the instrument; there are no samples.

**Download:** `frodhes-0.9.3.fwsc` below. **Try it first in your browser:** https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/

### Install
```sh
pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py frodhes-0.9.3.fwsc
```
If anything goes wrong, switch the FM-1 off, hold **OCT−** and switch it on to get the USB rescue, then install again.

### New in 0.9.3
- **VIBES, a vibraphone.** A fifth TYPE (ALGORITHM, or K1 on the main page): aluminium bars struck with mallets, each with its tuned tube, computed like the Rhodes (no samples).
  - VOICE becomes **STRIKE**, **MALLET** (soft, yarn, cord, rubber), **TUBE**, **WEIGHT**; DECAY becomes **RING**, **DAMPER**, **NOISE**, **TUNE**.
  - **The motor:** press **LFO**, turn K1 to **MOTOR** (after WAH). LFO again switches it off and on. Rate 0.5–10 Hz, or synced to the tempo.
  - Two new presets: **F13 VIBE MOTOR** and **F14 VIBES DRY** (program change 12 / 13; CC 79 picks VIBES).
  - It plays 2.5 dB louder than the Rhodes, so a struck bar doesn't sound weaker or closer to the jack's hiss.
  - The VOICE cards: STRIKE (where the mallet hits), MALLET (SOFT, YARN, CORD, RUBBER), TUBE (the resonator), WEIGHT (the mallet's mass); a harder strike is louder and brighter. DECAY: RING, DAMPER, NOISE (the mallet's tick and the damper's touch), TUNE. The MOTOR page (ON, DEPTH, SPEED, SHAPE; on by itself when the TYPE turns to VIBES, off on its K1 stays off).
- **FILTER** with its own **LFO** (two new pages): OFF, LP12, LP18, HP12, HP18, BP, NOTCH; 30 Hz–16 kHz, resonance, LFO up to ±4 octaves (sine, tri, saw, ramp, square, S&H; free or synced, free or retriggered by each note). PERFORM's K3 is now the filter's cutoff. CC 70–78.
- **MIDI clock:** CLOCK is INT or EXT (the default). On EXT the FM-1 follows clock from USB or TRS, and MIDI Start / Continue start the sequencer, Stop stops it.

### Changes
- **Page order:** PERFORM, FRODHES, VOICE, DECAY, then the effects, TEMPO ... LFO, ASSIGN, SETUP, MIDI. The instrument pages are together; ASSIGN moved next to SETUP.
- **Gauges** are a frame that fills, so an amount at 0 really looks empty (on the FM-1's screen the old dim bar looked full).
- **Factory presets level-matched:** F01–F12 all play at about F01's loudness, so browsing doesn't jump in volume.
- **The touch wah keeps its level:** a closed wah on a high chord no longer drops by up to 14 dB, and a low one no longer gets louder. It only colours the sound now.
- The CHORUS and PHASER **SPEED** cards show the rate that actually plays, 0.25–6 Hz (they showed twice the rate; the sound is the same).
- The **first boot** (or settings that can't be read) plays F01 STAGE 77, as the header says. It used to play bare defaults.
- **PHASER** no longer jumps in level when switched on: the bass is no longer lifted (the mix is lower and the feedback no longer resonates on the bass) and its two sides are 30° apart instead of 90°, so a mono sound does not suddenly turn very wide. It sounds a little different (closer to the middle, the low notes no longer boosted).
- **DRIVE** and **REVERB** have their own pages (DRIVE after FILTER: ON, AMOUNT, TONE, MIX; REVERB the last effect: MIX, DECAY, TONE, PRE-DELAY; CC 82–84, 92–94). The main page's middle cards belong to the engine: TONE / BELL on the Rhodes (DRIVE and REVERB moved to their pages), MALLET / MOTOR on VIBES.
- **Presets:** 64 parameters per preset (96-byte records); banks saved by 0.9.2 load and are converted.
- The audio engine is cheaper (the phaser and the Rhodes voices), with more headroom for VIBES.

### Fixes
- **DRIVE** was barely audible (a single note never reached the clipping); its top half now really drives (low settings, as the presets use, are unchanged).
- The **DELAY's echoes** came out weaker under the chorus or phaser; they keep their level now.
- The **DELAY** at high MIX x FEEDBACK grew forever (to the limiter); its loop gain is now under 1 at any setting.
- **LFO SYNC** set over MIDI (CC 77) kept the free rate.
- **PRESETS** turned past the last saved user preset jumped back to F14.
- **CHORD INVERSION 3RD** on a three-note chord played the 2ND again; it is the root position an octave up.
- The compressor's THRESH card showed "-0"; the manual described COMP ATTACK backwards and listed a COLOR setting that does not exist.
- **All Notes Off** (CC 123 / 120) left notes whose note-off got lost "held": that note was then dead and the arpeggiator kept playing it.
- A **burst of more than 32 notes** at once (a DAW sending a big chord, a fast pattern) lost the rest: missing or stuck notes.
- The tremolo's **SHAPE** above 1.4 made the gain chatter (F12 AM TREMOLO too).
- The cards **round their numbers** instead of cutting them (1.486 s showed 1.4S, now 1.5S; +11.8 dB showed +11, now +12).
- After undoing a preset load (hold SAVE) the header still named the preset just loaded.

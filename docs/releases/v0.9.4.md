**DRAFT (not released): Frodhes 0.9.4**: notes collected while MARIMBA is built. The numbers in the MARIMBA entries are
the design targets; they are filled in from the real tables (sync 2) and the FM-1 session (P5) before release.

### New
- **MARIMBA**, a sixth instrument TYPE: a concert marimba computed from the physics of rosewood bars tuned 1 : 4 : 10
  over their tubes, struck by yarn or rubber mallets. The low notes ring longer and bloom just after the strike (the
  long tubes), the top ones are short and dry; five authentic octaves, C2 to C7 (every MIDI note plays). No samples.
  It shares the vibraphone's engine and cards (VOICE 1/2: STRIKE, MALLET, TUBE, WEIGHT; VOICE 2/2: RING, MUTE,
  NOISE, TUNE); the MALLET card names the marimba's mallets: SOFT, MED, HARD, RUBBER.
- **MUTE** (MARIMBA's VOICE 2/2 K2, the same setting as VIBES's DAMPER, CC 103): a marimba has no damper, so the card
  is the player's hand at the key-up. 0 lets every note ring out (the factory MARIMBA presets start there); up, the
  hand stops the note sooner. The pedal still holds; All Notes Off still stops everything.
- No motor on MARIMBA: no MOTOR page (SELECT goes from VOICE 2/2 to COMPRESSOR); the FRODHES page's K3 is TUBE;
  the motor's CCs 47-51 change nothing there.
- Two factory presets, **F15 MARIMBA** and **F16 MRMB SOFT** (16 factory presets now); Program Change 14 / 15. Settings
  saved by 0.9.3 still point at the same preset (the stored preset index is converted once).

### Changed
- MIDI CC 79 (the bar TYPEs): 0..95 selects VIBES as before (0 and 64 included); **96..127 now selects MARIMBA**
  (in 0.9.3 every CC 79 value selected VIBES).

### Fixed
- A note held through a TYPE change to the other engine (a Rhodes type <-> VIBES / MARIMBA, or VIBES <-> MARIMBA),
  whose sound the change fades out, was silent when struck again before being let go (a second MIDI note-on, or an
  FM-1 key on a note a MIDI note still held); it now plays on the new type (0.9.3 had it too).

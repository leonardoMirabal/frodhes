**Frodhes 0.9.2**: a physically modelled Fender Rhodes electric piano for the M-VAVE FM-1. Every note is computed live from the mechanics of a Rhodes (hammer, tine, tonebar, pickup); there are no samples.

**Download:** `frodhes-0.9.2.fwsc` below. **Try it first in your browser:** https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/ (new look, with every button labelled)

### Install
```sh
pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py frodhes-0.9.2.fwsc
```
If anything goes wrong, switch the FM-1 off, hold **OCT−** and switch it on to get the USB rescue, then install again.

### New in 0.9.2
- **ENV** opens the **PHASER** (press again: on / off; the light shows it) and **SEL** opens the **DELAY** (press again: on / off).
- The **compressor's THRESH** is its switch: **OFF** at 0, turn it up and the compressor comes on, with makeup gain that keeps the volume the same.
- Chorus, decay and the compressor are reached with SELECT (chorus and delay can also be brought in from the PERFORM knobs).
- The **web emulator** looks like the FM-1, every button and knob is labelled with what it does, and the labels follow the page (for example PLAY = HOLD on the arpeggiator page). A short guide sits beside it.

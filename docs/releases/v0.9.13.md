**Frodhes 0.9.13**: a physically modelled electric piano, vibraphone and marimba for the M-VAVE FM-1, the Ableton Move, the Akai MPC and your computer. Every note is computed live from the mechanics of the instrument; there are no samples.

**Download:** `frodhes.fwsc` below (the same file as `frodhes-0.9.13.fwsc`; the newest is always at https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/releases/latest/download/frodhes.fwsc). **Try it first in your browser:** https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/

### Install

**FM-1**: download `frodhes.fwsc` below. Install it from the [emulator page](https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/#install) (Chrome or Edge, **Install on your FM-1 or SMK-37**), or from a terminal:

```sh
pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py frodhes.fwsc
```

Your settings, presets and pattern are kept. If anything goes wrong, switch the FM-1 off, hold **OCT−** and switch it on: that is the USB rescue, and you can install again from there.

**Computer (plugin)**: `frodhes-plugin-macos.pkg` (or `frodhes-plugin-macos.zip`), `frodhes-plugin-windows.zip`, `frodhes-plugin-linux-x86_64.zip` or `frodhes-plugin-linux-aarch64.zip` below. How to install each: [PLUGIN.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/PLUGIN.md#packages).

**Move**: you need Schwung on the Move. Install Frodhes with Schwung Manager in a web browser: **Install Custom Module > From GitHub URL**, `leonardoMirabal/frodhes`. Or download `frodhes-module.tar.gz` below and use **From Tarball**. Details: [the Move manual](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/MOVE.md#install).

**Akai MPC**: `frodhes-mpc.zip` below (MPC OS 3.10.1 or later, External Plugin Support on). Copy its folder `Frodhes - VST - Frodhes` into `VST2 Plugins` at the top of the MPC's drive. Details: [MPC.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/MPC.md#install-and-remove).

**SMK-37 Pro, SMK-37 Elite, MKE-P37**: `frodhes-smk37-pro.fwsc`, `frodhes-smk37-elite.fwsc` and `frodhes-mke-p37.fwsc` below. Read [SMK37.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/SMK37.md) first.

### New in 0.9.13
- Akai MPC: Frodhes can be installed and updated from the community's Open MPC plugin catalogue with its Plugin
  Manager, which picks the build for your MPC. Each release now also has the catalogue's two packages, one for the
  32-bit and one for the 64-bit MPC models. Installing by hand from the release's zip works as before.

### Fixed
- ARPEGGIO page: after ARP GATE was set over MIDI to a value between the 5 % steps (such as 73 %), turning **GATE**
  kept the odd value (78 %, 83 % ...), so the round steps could not be reached. Now the first step of the knob lands
  on the 5 % step in the direction you turn, then it steps 5 %.
- TEMPO page: after a tapped tempo or a MIDI clock left the tempo between whole numbers (such as 120.1 BPM), turning
  **BPM** kept the tenth (119.1, 121.1), so 120 could not be set again by hand. Now a slow turn steps 0.1 BPM, so
  120.1 turns down to 120.0 and every tenth can be set by hand; a fast turn moves 1 to 4 BPM a click. The plugin's
  BPM - / + keys step 0.1 BPM too (Shift with the arrow keys: 1 BPM).
- With a MIDI clock running (CLOCK on EXT, a computer or a drum machine sending it), setup changes such as the MIDI
  channel, the display or a tapped tempo were not saved until the clock stopped, so switching off first lost them.
  Now they are saved as usual, about 2 s after the change once the sound has died away, and the sequencer's pattern
  too while the sequencer is stopped. The clock keeps its tempo and its beat through the save.
- FILTER: at a very low FREQ the filter could keep a faint hum going after the notes ended (pulsing with TREM), so the
  sound never went fully silent and saves kept waiting. The tail now ends in silence.
- REVERB INFINITE: the INFINITE reverb could build up to full level at low DECAY or a dark TONE and stay there, even
  after the reverb was switched OFF; fixed. Its tail now always fades at the DECAY you set, and INFINITE picked again
  after its tail has ended starts fresh.
- Akai MPC: the knob pictures on the plugin's pages were taller than the largest picture the MPC loads. They are now
  within that size; the knobs look as before and their pictures move in slightly coarser steps (105 instead of 127).
- Akai MPC: installed with the open-mpc Plugin Manager (version 1.2.0 and older), Frodhes did not show up in the
  plugin browser: the manager pointed the MPC at the plugin's binary by its bare file name, and the binary sat in a
  subfolder. The two binaries (32-bit and 64-bit) are now at the top of the plugin folder, where every installer
  looks. Installing by hand: replace the whole folder "Frodhes - VST - Frodhes" (delete the old one first).
- Desktop plugin: the BPM control's buttons, arrow keys and drag now share the tempo's own range, 40 to 240 BPM.
  Dragging past either end no longer leaves a stretch of dead travel before the value moves back.
- Presets: turning PRESETS and pressing SAVE within a moment could load the preset you had turned to just after the
  save (or after HOME cancelled it), so a different sound played than the one you had just saved. Now the sound you
  saved, or the one you had, keeps playing. A Program Change arriving while you browse no longer has it either.
- RESPONSE B: on VIBES, playing harder could come out slightly softer between soft and medium touches; fixed. On
  VIBES and MARIMBA, B now lifts the soft end smoothly, so every harder strike is louder, with the same tone.
- DELAY: after one note through ANA, every later turn of TIME glided for many seconds (39 ms to 1.17 s took about
  18 s) and the next echoes came bunched and bent. The delay now comes fully to rest once its last echo has died away,
  and TIME then jumps straight to the new value, as it always did before any note.
- TUNE turned while notes were held: the held notes jumped to the new tuning at the next pitch-bend message. A note now
  keeps the TUNE it was struck with; the bend moves it by the bend alone.
- Pitch bend on a big chord: with more than four notes sounding, the notes followed the wheel in steps and up to 20
  cents apart from each other. Every sounding note now follows the wheel together.
- Half pedal (CC 64 between 42 and 63): the key-up knock and the felt's sound came at full strength, as with the pedal
  up. They now follow how lightly the dampers touch.
- Playing more notes than there are voices: the cut of the oldest note, when a new one takes its voice, is smoother
  (a loud low note no longer ticks under a soft new one); the new note starts as early as before.
- TREM/WAH: switching the effect off with its **EFFECT** card forgot which one was on, so pressing LFO twice (or
  turning its depth up on PERFORM) brought back PAN instead of the WAH or TREM you had. Now it brings back the one
  that was on.

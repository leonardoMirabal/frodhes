**Frodhes 0.9.14** adds WHURLI, a reed electric piano that growls when you strike hard, with four presets; the TYPE controller (CC 80) now reaches all five electric pianos; EARLY FELT and BELL DYNO are evened out across the keyboard; and the desktop plugin gets a drive of its own, WARM.

**Download:** `frodhes.fwsc` below (the same file as `frodhes-0.9.14.fwsc`; the newest is always at https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/releases/latest/download/frodhes.fwsc). **Try it first in your browser:** https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/

### Install

**FM-1**: download `frodhes.fwsc` below. Install it from the [emulator page](https://leonardomirabal.github.io/frodhes/#install) (Chrome or Edge, **Install on your FM-1 or SMK-37**), or from a terminal:

```sh
pip install mido python-rtmidi
python3 tools/fm1_install.py frodhes.fwsc
```

Your settings, presets and pattern are kept. If anything goes wrong, switch the FM-1 off, hold **OCT−** and switch it on: that is the USB rescue, and you can install again from there.

**Computer (plugin)**: `frodhes-plugin-macos.pkg` (or `frodhes-plugin-macos.zip`), `frodhes-plugin-windows.zip`, `frodhes-plugin-linux-x86_64.zip` or `frodhes-plugin-linux-aarch64.zip` below. How to install each: [PLUGIN.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/PLUGIN.md#packages).

**Move**: you need Schwung on the Move. Install Frodhes with Schwung Manager in a web browser: **Install Custom Module > From GitHub URL**, `leonardoMirabal/frodhes`. Or download `frodhes-module.tar.gz` below and use **From Tarball**. Details: [the Move manual](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/MOVE.md#install).

**Akai MPC**: `frodhes-mpc.zip` below (MPC OS 3.10.1 or later, External Plugin Support on). Copy its folder `Frodhes - VST - Frodhes` into `VST2 Plugins` at the top of the MPC's drive. Details: [MPC.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/MPC.md#install-and-remove).

**SMK-37 Pro, SMK-37 Elite, MKE-P37**: `frodhes-smk37-pro.fwsc`, `frodhes-smk37-elite.fwsc` and `frodhes-mke-p37.fwsc` below. Read [SMK37.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/SMK37.md) first.

### New in 0.9.14
- **WHURLI**, a reed electric piano, joins the electric pianos: a thin steel reed, a bright and slightly hollow tone, and a
  throaty growl when you strike hard. BARK sets how much growl, HAMMER how soon it starts and how sharp it is, and RESPONSE
  how hard you have to play to get it. Built from physics, no samples. Its keys run from A1 to C7.
  ALGORITHM scrolls to it right after BELL DYNO, before VIBES and MARIMBA; saved sounds and MIDI keep their meaning.
- Four factory presets for it: **F20 WHURLI** (dry), **F21 REED GROWL**, **F22 REED TREM** (the tremolo piano) and
  **F23 REED SOFT**. On the FM-1, program changes 35 to 38 load them.

### Changes
- FM-1: the factory presets are now F01-F23, and your own presets stay U01-U16 after them. The first time the new
  firmware starts, it moves your presets up four places by itself, so every saved sound, your setup and the sound the
  FM-1 was left on keep their place. Nothing to do.
- On the Move, the desktop plugin and the MPC every preset number stays as it was: the four WHURLI presets are added
  after the others, as F58-F61 (on the Move, program changes 73 to 76).
- The electric pianos' TYPE controller, CC 80, now picks the five pianos in five equal bands: 0-25 STAGE 77, 26-51
  SUITCASE, 52-76 EARLY FELT, 77-102 BELL DYNO and 103-127 WHURLI. It had four bands: 0-31 STAGE 77, 32-63 SUITCASE,
  64-95 EARLY FELT and 96-127 BELL DYNO. The values 0, 32, 64 and 96 pick the same piano as before. 26-31, 52-63,
  77-95 and 103-127 now pick the next piano along, and 127 picks WHURLI instead of BELL DYNO. The same on the FM-1,
  the Move, the desktop plugin and the MPC.
- Picking a piano with CC 80 now does what turning to it on the screen does: WHURLI starts from its own neutral voice.
- Desktop plugin: the drive's first type is now **WARM**, a warm, smooth overdrive of the plugin's own, in place of
  FDRV. It has the same knobs, the same CC 84 values and the same place in the presets (a preset with FDRV plays WARM).
  The other four drive types are unchanged. The FM-1, the Move and the MPC keep FDRV.
- Desktop plugin: the delay's code was rewritten. Its output is the same as before, sample for sample.
- Desktop plugin: click the lamp on an effect's tab to switch it on or off; it comes back with the setting it had.

### Fixed
- **EARLY FELT**: the small jumps in loudness from one key to the next where the hammers change (between D3 and D#3,
  C4 and C#4, A#4 and B4, C6 and C#6) are gone, most audible in soft playing. The keys either side now blend into each
  other, and the felt sound is kept.
- **BELL DYNO**: a single hard note no longer hits the top of the output. With TONE below the middle it now keeps the
  same loudness against STAGE 77 as at the middle. Its top keys (E7 and up) no longer get louder and louder toward C8.
  With BELL near its top, the bell in the low notes is a little softer.

  With TONE low and BELL high together, hard bass notes no longer hit the top of the output: there BELL adds less
  above its middle. The small jump in loudness between F#3 and G3, and between C4 and C#4, in hard playing is gone.

## New

- **Frodhes on the Move**: Frodhes now runs on the Ableton Move as a sound generator in Schwung.
  - **All six instruments and every effect**, with 16 voices and the higher-quality version of each instrument.
  - **The reverb at the full sample rate**: its tails keep their top octave.
  - **Frodhes' own screen**: the eight knobs as eight cards, a page at a time, in the FM-1's order. Touch or turn a
    knob to see its card large. A double tap on a knob sets it back to its default. The knob lights show the values.
  - **The arrows**: Left turns the effects off or on (the compressor stays on), Right turns the sustain on or off. A
    sustain pedal on CC 64 works as on the FM-1.
  - **Presets**: F01-F19 are the FM-1's, F20-F57 are 38 more for the Move, and 16 user slots. SAVE TO USER, in the
    list under the jog wheel, saves your sound.
  - **The arpeggiator**, with HOLD. It follows the Move's tempo and transport, as do all synced rates. The Move's
    sequencer and scales play Frodhes.
  - **Pressure mappings**: the pads' pressure can drive a control through its CC number. Off by default.
  - **Up to four Frodhes at once**, one per track, each with its own sound. Three play without dropouts. With four,
    very heavy playing with every effect on can drop audio.
  - To play at every velocity, turn the Move's **Full Velocity** setting off. Keep Schwung's slot volume at 100 %.
  - **Install**: you need Schwung on the Move. Install Frodhes with Schwung Manager in a web browser: **Install
    Custom Module > From GitHub URL**, `leonardoMirabal/frodhes`. Or download `frodhes-module.tar.gz` and use **From
    Tarball**. Details: [the Move manual](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/MOVE.md#install).

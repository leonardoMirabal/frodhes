## Changes

- **Touch wah**: far less noise on soft notes, less grit on loud ones, and the sweep no longer flickers on a held
  note.
- **Chorus**: cleaner on high notes, and turning DEPTH quickly no longer clicks.
- **Delay ANA and TAPE**: cleaner echoes, and each repeat keeps more of its top.
- **Filter with its LFO, and the phaser**: smoother sweeps, without the faint grain of the fast settings.
- **LEVEL**: presets turned down or up with LEVEL keep a clean, quiet tail.

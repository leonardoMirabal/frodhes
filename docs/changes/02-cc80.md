## Changes

- The electric pianos' TYPE controller, CC 80, now picks the five pianos in five equal bands: 0-25 STAGE 77, 26-51
  SUITCASE, 52-76 EARLY FELT, 77-102 BELL DYNO and 103-127 WHURLI. It had four bands: 0-31 STAGE 77, 32-63 SUITCASE,
  64-95 EARLY FELT and 96-127 BELL DYNO. The values 0, 32, 64 and 96 pick the same piano as before. 26-31, 52-63,
  77-95 and 103-127 now pick the next piano along, and 127 picks WHURLI instead of BELL DYNO. The same on the FM-1,
  the Move, the desktop plugin and the MPC.
- Picking a piano with CC 80 now does what turning to it on the screen does: WHURLI starts from its own neutral voice.

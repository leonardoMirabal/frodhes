## New

- **WHURLI**, a reed electric piano, joins the electric pianos: a thin steel reed, a bright and slightly hollow tone, and a
  throaty growl when you strike hard. BARK sets how much growl, HAMMER how soon it starts and how sharp it is, and RESPONSE
  how hard you have to play to get it. Built from physics, no samples. Its keys run from A1 to C7.
- Four factory presets for it: **F20 WHURLI** (dry), **F21 REED GROWL**, **F22 REED TREM** (the tremolo piano) and
  **F23 REED SOFT**. Program changes 35 to 38 load them.

## Changes

- The factory presets are now F01-F23, so your own U01-U16 sit after F23: a saved sound, a saved setup or the sound an
  FM-1 was left on keeps its place (the numbers move up by four once, when the new firmware first starts). On the Move,
  the plugin and the MPC the extra presets are now F24-F61 (they were F20-F57), and on the Move their program changes are 39-76 (they were 35-72).

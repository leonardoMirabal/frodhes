## Changes

- Wider dynamics: soft and medium notes on the electric piano are quieter, so the keys answer your touch over a wider
  range; the loudest notes are unchanged. RESPONSE LIGHT keeps the old feel.
- VIBES and MARIMBA come down with it, so switching sounds or presets keeps the balance you know. Only their volume
  changes: the compressor, DRIVE, the touch wah and the tape delay respond to them exactly as before.

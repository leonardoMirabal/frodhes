## New

- **PLAY's beat light can be switched off** ([#29](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/issues/29)). The
  DISPLAY page has a fourth card, PLAY LED: BEAT (the default) blinks PLAY on the beat as before, OFF keeps its
  light dark. Every other light is unchanged. It is kept with the settings, so it stays off after a power cycle.

## New

- DISPLAY LEDS: a fifth mode, **SCALE**, after ALL. As ALL, but only the keys of the scale are lit: with SNAP the keys whose own note is in the scale (the snapped keys stay dark), with DEGREE the white keys. A played key flips (a scale key goes dark, another key lights). With no scale on it looks as ALL. Saved LEDS settings keep their meaning.
- DISPLAY COLOR: **RED**, a full red, appended after MANGO (27 steps). Saved COLOR settings keep their colour.

## Fixed

- Key lights under the sustain pedal: a key let go before the pedal went down no longer lights again when the pedal goes down (its note was only in its silent damped tail, or a MARIMBA bar ringing on). Under the pedal a key stays lit only for a note the pedal holds: one held when the pedal went down, or played while it was down, as long as it still sounds. Rhodes, VIBES and MARIMBA.

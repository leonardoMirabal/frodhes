## On your computer: the desktop plugin

Frodhes also runs in your music software on a Mac, as a CLAP, VST3 or Audio Unit instrument.

- The same instruments, effects and factory sounds, computed by the same code. [TODO: "The higher-quality version
  with 16 voices, as on the Move; ENGINE switches to CLASSIC, the FM-1's own sound."]
- The host's tempo drives the synced effects. Any sample rate. Up to 16 instances in one project.
- A sound saved in the plugin loads on the Move, and the other way round. [TODO: confirm]
- [TODO: "Its own window: the FM-1's pages and cards." or "No window of its own yet: your host shows every control
  with its full name."]
- macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. Windows comes later.

Install:

1. Download [`frodhes-plugin-macos.zip`](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/releases/latest/download/frodhes-plugin-macos.zip)
   [TODO: or the .pkg: "and open it; it installs the three formats for every user on the Mac."].
2. Copy the format your host uses into its folder (create the folder if it is not there):

   | Format | File | Folder |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | CLAP | `Frodhes.clap` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/` |
   | VST3 | `Frodhes.vst3` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/` |
   | Audio Unit | `Frodhes.component` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/` |

   (`~/Library` is the Library folder in your home folder: in the Finder, **Go > Go to Folder...** and paste the path.
   `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/...` works too, for every user on the Mac.)
3. [GATEKEEPER, only if the bundles are NOT notarized:] macOS blocks a plugin downloaded from the internet that is
   not notarized. Clear the download flag once, in Terminal:
   ```sh
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/*/Frodhes.*
   ```
   [if notarized: drop step 3, or keep one line: "The plugin is signed and notarized: macOS opens it without a
   warning."]
4. Restart your music software, or let it rescan its plugins. It appears as **Frodhes**, an instrument. For the Audio
   Unit, a restart of the Mac (or `killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar` in Terminal) makes macOS see it at once.

To remove it, delete the three `Frodhes.*` files from those folders. More: [the manual](docs/MANUAL.md#17-appendix-the-desktop-plugin).

## New

- Akai MPC: `frodhes-mpc.zip`, an instrument plugin for MPC standalone hardware (MPC OS 3.10.1 or later, External
  Plugin Support on). The desktop plugin's engine: electric piano, vibraphone and marimba, the same effects, 16 voices
  on every instrument, the factory presets F01-F57 as programs. Checked on an MPC by a tester. Its screen is the
  desktop plugin's look: a brushed-aluminium plate round a black body, eight pages of two panels, each panel four
  large knobs or switches that one group of Q-Links plays, the sections' switches and lamps in the panels' headers.
  Install, what to report and how to build it in
  [docs/MPC.md](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/blob/main/docs/MPC.md).

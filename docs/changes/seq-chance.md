## New

- **CHANCE per step in the sequencer** ([#30](https://github.com/leonardoMirabal/frodhes/issues/30)). A new page,
  SEQUENCER 2, right after SEQUENCER: K1 STEP picks a step (the same cursor ALGORITHM moves on SEQUENCER, shown as
  bar.beat.16th, such as 2.3.4), K2 CHANCE sets how often the notes starting on it play on each pass of the loop,
  from 100 % (the default) down to 10 % in 10 % steps ("-" when nothing starts there). A step's chord plays whole or
  not at all, and each start of the sequencer rolls anew. CHANCE is saved with the pattern; patterns made before
  play exactly as before. FM-1 and SMK-37.

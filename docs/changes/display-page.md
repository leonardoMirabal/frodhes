## New
- **DISPLAY page** (GLO four times): **COLOR**, the screen's colour, AMBER as before and then 25 pastel hues around
  the colour wheel, from PEACH through the pinks, violets, blues, cyans and greens to LEMON, GOLD and MANGO;
  **BRIGHTNESS**, for a dark room: the screen and every light dimmed together, 10 to 1; **LEDS**, which keys light
  (none, the played keys, the played keys and the scale's root keys glowing faintly, or every key lit for playing in
  the dark, a played key going dark). Kept with the settings. After an update the FM-1 looks as before: AMBER,
  BRIGHTNESS 10 and ROOTS are 0.9.3's screen and key lights.
- **Notes over MIDI light their keys**, from USB and the TRS jack, as the FM-1's own keys do.

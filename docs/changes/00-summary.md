## Summary

runs the FM-1 on both cores with 14 voices in one package and adds Frodhes for the Akai MPC. It also brings the
reverb back to its full level on HALL and INFINITE, gives SUITCASE its own clean, round voice again, and adds a
cleaner upper register on every electric piano, a clearer MARIMBA mallet tick at high NOISE, richer soft notes on
the Move, the plugin and the Akai MPC, a new MIX law on DELAY, DRIVE and REVERB, a fuller velocity response on the
electric piano, update notices in the desktop plugin, and a web emulator that plays the FM-1's 14-voice two-core
sound.

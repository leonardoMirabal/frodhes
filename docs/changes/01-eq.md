## New

- **EQ page**, between COMPRESSOR and DRIVE: a LOW shelf at 120 Hz, a MID band you can move from 200 Hz to 5 kHz
  (MID FREQ), and a HIGH shelf at 5 kHz, each ±15 dB. It sits before the drive, so it also changes how the drive
  breaks up. It is saved in presets. Presets and settings from earlier versions load with it flat, and flat it
  leaves the sound untouched. Over MIDI: CC 34-37.

## Changes

- **Drive**: FDRV and KHLON are cleaner when driven hard on high notes, with far less harsh, out-of-tune edge.

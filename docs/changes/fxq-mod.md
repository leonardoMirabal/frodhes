## Changes

- TREM/WAH, the touch wah: the same filter with the same response (within 0.1 dB at every frequency, the top octave included), now computed 64 times finer inside with finer coefficients, and the make-up gain applied before the output is rounded. The old one's rounding gave quiet notes a noise floor only 25 dB under the note (a 1 kHz tone at -41 dBFS through a closed, resonant wah); now it is 50 dB under, the limit set by the input's own grain. Louder notes lost their grit: up to 25 dB less distortion (a high C at full velocity: its 3rd to 9th harmonics were mostly the old filter's own distortion, 12 to 28 dB over what the note puts through). The sweep keeps its range (300 Hz to 2.5 kHz) and its make-up. The cutoff no longer flickers between two of its steps on a held note. The level on chords and single notes is within 0.35 dB of before.
- CHORUS: the moving delay is read with a 4-point interpolation instead of a straight line between two samples: images of the high notes 6 to 18 dB lower, and no DC left after the sound stops. Turning DEPTH now glides both the mix and the sweep's width (turning it from 127 to 0 in one go was a click). With the second core the right side runs on it.
- DELAY, ANA and TAPE: the wow and flutter read the line with the same 4-point interpolation (images of the echo's high notes 6 to 12 dB lower; each repeat keeps more of its top). DIG is unchanged.
- FILTER: with the LFO sweeping, the coefficients follow every 8 samples instead of every 32 (images of a tone 28 to 34 dB lower with the fastest sweep).
- PHASER: the sweep moves sample by sample instead of every 32 samples (images 40 dB lower), and no DC is left after the sound stops.
- LEVEL below or above its middle: the output is dithered where LEVEL scales it, so the quiet tails of presets with a LEVEL trim keep the engine's clean decay (harmonics of a -64 dBFS tone 12 dB lower at LEVEL 40).

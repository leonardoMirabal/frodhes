## Changes

- TREM/WAH, the touch wah: its filter is now the FILTER page's kind (a state-variable filter that stays stable at any setting), computed 16 times finer inside, with the make-up gain applied before the output is rounded. The old one's rounding gave quiet notes a noise floor only 25 dB under the note (a 1 kHz tone at -41 dBFS through a closed, resonant wah); now it is 44 dB under, at the limit set by the input's own grain. Loud notes lost their grit too, up to 19 dB less distortion. The sweep keeps its range (300 Hz to 2.5 kHz) and its make-up; the level on chords and single notes is within 0.3 dB of before. Its top octave (above 12 kHz) is a little darker, where the wah already passes little.
- CHORUS: the moving delay is read with a 4-point interpolation instead of a straight line between two samples: images of the high notes 12 to 19 dB lower, and no DC left after the sound stops. Turning DEPTH now glides both the mix and the sweep's width (turning it from 127 to 0 in one go was a click). With the second core the right side runs on it.
- DELAY, ANA and TAPE: the wow and flutter read the line with the same 4-point interpolation (images of the echo's high notes 6 to 12 dB lower; each repeat keeps more of its top). DIG is unchanged.
- FILTER: with the LFO sweeping, the coefficients follow every 8 samples instead of every 32 (images of a tone 28 to 34 dB lower with the fastest sweep).
- PHASER: the sweep moves sample by sample instead of every 32 samples (images 40 dB lower), and no DC is left after the sound stops.
- LEVEL below or above its middle: the output is dithered where LEVEL scales it, so the quiet tails of presets with a LEVEL trim keep the engine's clean decay (harmonics of a -64 dBFS tone 12 dB lower at LEVEL 40).

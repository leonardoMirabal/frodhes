mpc/sdk/: three header files of the MPC Plugin SDK (mpc-plugin-builder-sdk), copied unchanged from its
framework/ folder:

    mpc_plugin.h  mpc_vst2_abi.h  mpc_resources.h

Source:   https://github.com/inmusic-software/mpc-plugin-builder-sdk
Commit:   febd2ca5bd9eb16c4a412837196e145566bc981b  (mpc/build.py SDK_COMMIT; the build checks the copies against it)
Licence:  MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 inMusic Brands, Inc. (LICENSE in this folder)

They are the only SDK files compiled into the plugin. The SDK's tools (the host simulator, the skin generator, the
validator) are used at build time from a checkout of the same commit in .local/mpc-sdk (mpc/build.py fetches it);
none of them is part of the plugin. The rest of mpc/ and everything it compiles from the Frodhes repository is
GPL-3.0-only.
